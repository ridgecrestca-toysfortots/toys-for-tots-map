Toy Donation Guidelines

Priority: Ages 0 to 2

Teethers, sensory rattles, building blocks, board books, and developmental infant toys.

Priority: Ages 10 to 12 (Females)

Craft kits, art sets, headphones, board games, beauty accessories, and sports gear.

Important Requirements

All toys must be new and unwrapped. Blankets are not accepted. Please call sites prior to drop-off.
