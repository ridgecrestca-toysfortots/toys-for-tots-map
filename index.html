Age Range & Gift Guidelines

Serving Children Ages 0 to 12

Help us ensure all children receive gifts this season. Pay special attention to our highest-need age brackets!

Most Needed Gift Categories

Infants & Toddlers (Ages 0–2): Baby gear, sensory toys, blocks • Pre-Teens (Ages 10–12 Females): Craft kits, toiletries/beauty gift sets, sports equipment, board games, headphones.

Great Toys to Donate

  • Infant developmental toys & blankets (0–2)
  • Pre-teen female gift sets & crafts (10–12)
  • Board games & puzzle sets
  • Sports balls & outdoor activities
  • Books & art kits

Items Cannot Be Accepted

  • Used or opened toys
  • Realistic toy weapons or guns
  • Food or candy items
  • Liquids or chemical items (slimes)

Packaging Advice

Please leave all items unwrapped! Volunteers need to inspect and categorize toys by age and gender group before distribution.

Pro Tip: Batteries included with toys are extra appreciated!
